Eligibility

  • Open to eligible participants from India and other countries.
  • Students, researchers, professionals, farmers, artisans, photographers and the general public may participate.
  • Participants must provide accurate information.

What Can Be Submitted?

  • Bamboo forests and plantations
  • Bamboo biodiversity and landscapes
  • Bamboo farmers and livelihoods
  • Bamboo architecture and products
  • Bamboo crafts and traditional practices
  • Bamboo technology and innovation
  • Bamboo and climate/environment

Submission Specifications

  • Submit one original photograph.
  • JPG/JPEG format is preferred.
  • Image should be clear and reasonably high resolution.
  • Basic cropping and colour/brightness correction are permitted.
  • Misleading manipulation or composite images should not be submitted as ordinary photographs.
  • Provide photograph title and description as requested in the form.

📋 Common Competition Rules

  1. Participants may participate in multiple competitions, subject to the individual competition rules.
  2. All entries must be original and submitted by the participant.
  3. Participants are responsible for obtaining permission for third-party copyrighted material used in their entry.
  4. Plagiarism, fraudulent submissions, copyright infringement, offensive content or false information may result in disqualification.
  5. Participants must provide accurate contact information.
  6. The final online submission deadline is 11 September 2026, unless a specific competition announcement states otherwise.
  7. Late submissions may not be considered.
  8. Selected entries may be showcased through official World Bamboo Week/BRTC communication platforms, subject to applicable permissions.
  9. The authorised judging committee's decision shall be final and binding.
  10. Certificates and physical bamboo goodies are subject to eligibility verification, applicable competition rules, availability and logistical arrangements.
