About the Week

One Week. One Bamboo Vision.

A high-visibility hybrid platform designed to connect knowledge with people and bamboo with opportunity.

Bamboo for the Future

World Bamboo Week 2026 is proposed as a seven-day national and international platform hosted by BRTC, Chichpalli. It brings together science, research, technology, education, livelihoods, entrepreneurship, culture, industry and public participation.

  • National and international knowledge exchange
  • Research, laboratory, nursery and technology showcase
  • Farmer, artisan, youth, student and entrepreneur engagement
  • Digital and on-site competitions
  • Exhibitions, demonstrations, workshops and BRTC Bamboo Trail
  • World Bamboo Day flagship celebration on 18 September
AwarenessPromote bamboo as a sustainable, versatile and future-oriented resource.
Science & TechnologyShare bamboo research, propagation, processing, construction and product innovation.
LivelihoodsCreate opportunities for farmers, artisans, SHGs, entrepreneurs and rural communities.
Youth & EducationEngage students, teachers, creators and institutions through learning and competitions.
Global VisibilityExpand BRTC's digital and institutional visibility across India and international bamboo networks.
PartnershipsBuild responsible collaboration with industry, CSR, research and knowledge partners.

Programme Components

Online Knowledge + On-Site Experience

World Bamboo Week combines digital reach with direct engagement at BRTC.

🌏 Online Activities

  • 01. Bamboo Expert Webinar Series — national and international expert talks on science, technology, cultivation and industry.
  • 02. Bamboo Farmers' Digital Training — species selection, plantation, harvesting, treatment and markets.
  • 03. Bamboo Success Story Series — farmers, artisans, entrepreneurs, designers and innovators.
  • 04. Bamboo Around the World – Global Showcase — bamboo practices and knowledge from different countries.
  • 05. #BambooWeek Digital Pledge Campaign — public sustainability action and bamboo pledge.

🏛️ On-Site Activities at BRTC

  • 06. Bamboo Technology Exhibition — products, technologies, research outputs and prototypes.
  • 07. Live Bamboo Craft Demonstration — traditional processing and craft techniques.
  • 08. Bamboo Construction Demonstration — joints, treatment, structural elements and assembly.
  • 09. Bamboo Food & Culinary Experience — appropriate bamboo food applications with hygiene controls.
  • 10. Bamboo Skill Workshop — participants make a simple bamboo product.
  • 11. Bamboo Innovation Walk / BRTC Bamboo Trail — nursery, species, laboratories, products, research and technologies.
  • 12. Adopt a Bamboo Plant — dedicated awareness/adoption spots and participant records.

Governance & Committee Structure

The formal committee order is subject to competent institutional approval. Officer/member names not yet authorised are intentionally not presented as confirmed public appointments.

01. Steering Committee

Strategic direction, institutional approvals, major partners/sponsors, milestone review and final closure.

02. Core Organising Committee

Day-to-day command, master action plan, responsibility matrix, issue escalation and integration of all activities.

03. Programme & Academic

Seven-day session architecture, experts, moderators, webinars, training and scientific quality.

04. Competition

All 10 competitions, rules, entry screening, jury coordination, results and competition archive.

05. Digital, Website & IT

Website, Google Forms, master links, livestreaming, digital communication, backups and technical support.

06. Sponsorship & Partnerships

CSR, industry, institutional and knowledge partners; sponsor pipeline, proposals and fulfilment tracking.

07. Finance, Procurement & Accounts

Budget control, procurement, quotations, bills, prize/certificate expenditure and financial closure.

08. Exhibition & Demonstration

Technology exhibition, craft, construction, workshops, display fabrication and BRTC Bamboo Trail.

09. Hospitality & Protocol

Invitations, reception, seating, refreshments, guest kits, mementos and approved transport coordination.

10. Publicity, Media & Outreach

Branding, press notes, social calendar, media invitations, institutional outreach and visibility.

11. Venue, Logistics & Safety

Venue, utilities, power, internet, sanitation, crowd movement, first aid and demonstration safety.

12. Documentation & Reporting

Photography, video, attendance, daily reports, media archive, final narrative and impact report.

13. Volunteer & Student Cell

Volunteers, help desks, participant support, competitions, exhibition, trail and hospitality assistance.

14. Awards, Certificates & Recognition

Winner lists, certificates, trophies/mementos, bamboo goodies and verified recognition records.
