About the Week

Bamboo. Knowledge. Opportunity.

A seven-day platform connecting people, institutions, science, technology, livelihoods and the future of bamboo.

Hosted at BRTC, Chichpalli

World Bamboo Week 2026 is proposed as a high-visibility national and international hybrid programme at the Bamboo Research & Training Centre, Chichpalli.

The programme combines online and physical competitions, expert sessions, exhibitions, demonstrations, workshops, research and technology showcases, farmer and artisan engagement, entrepreneurship, youth participation, media outreach and institutional networking.

  • ✓ Science, research and bamboo technology
  • ✓ Education, youth and student participation
  • ✓ Farmers, SHGs, artisans and rural livelihoods
  • ✓ Entrepreneurship, startups and industry
  • ✓ National and international digital outreach

Our Vision

To position BRTC, Chichpalli as a recognised national platform for bamboo science, technology, education, innovation, entrepreneurship, culture, sustainable livelihoods and green development.
